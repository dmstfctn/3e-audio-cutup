Click a swatch, or anywhere on a photo, to add its colour to that photo's list for the effect picked above (+1, flash or strip). Shift-click adds it to the defaults instead. Drag a colour onto any list to add it there, or along its list to reorder it: the flash cycles through its list in order. success and fail are one colour each (a pick replaces it): shapes found, the border of a photo picked right or wrong, and a miss's ring. Click a colour in a list to take it out. A photo with an empty list uses the defaults, shown faded. Pair it is one card: its colours come from all its photos, and every pair matched uses its lists.
